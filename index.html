Before you subscribe

A few useful answers.

When will my email arrive?

We send the daily briefing at 8 am London time during British Summer Time and 7 am during GMT. The website displays the current London delivery time. Email arrival can vary slightly by provider.

Which matches are included?

The briefing covers fixtures for which sufficient data is available. Coverage varies with the day's schedule and available information. See coverage and sources.

Is this a tipping service?

No. Your subscription pays for statistical analysis of football fixtures. Nothing in it is advice or a recommendation to act, and no forecast is a statement about what will happen.

How do I unsubscribe or stop paying?

Both at once, in one click. The Unsubscribe link at the foot of any Sharper Side email takes you off the list, and because Buttondown handles the billing as well as the list, it ends the renewal too. With no email to hand, the subscriber portal does the same. The detail is here.

Analysis, not advice.

Sharper Side is statistical analysis of football fixtures, published for information only. It is not advice of any kind and nothing in it is a recommendation to act, financially or otherwise. Football is uncertain, and no forecast here is a statement about what will happen.
