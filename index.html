Three percentages that add to a hundred, then what moved them. A side rated 54% loses or draws almost half the time, so the strongest outcome is named as the strongest reading and never as a result. Where the data does not separate two teams, that is reported as the finding rather than resolved into a name.

Each fixture also carries the case against its own strongest reading wherever recent form contradicts it. An analyst who lists only the supporting facts is not analysing, and a reader who spots the contradiction unaided is right to stop trusting the rest.
