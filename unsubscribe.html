Subscription help

Leaving should be straightforward.

One click in any Sharper Side email stops everything — the daily email and, if you are paying, the renewal with it. There is no form to fill in and nobody to persuade.

The quickest way

Every Sharper Side email carries an Unsubscribe link at the foot of it. One click takes you off the list. Buttondown handles both the list and the billing, so unsubscribing from the newsletter ends the recurring payment at the same time — there is no second place to go and nothing left running afterwards.

Most email apps also show an unsubscribe control of their own, next to the sender’s name at the top of the message. That one does the same thing, and it is quicker if the email is already open.

No Sharper Side email to hand?

Use the subscriber portal instead. Enter the address you subscribed with and Buttondown emails you a link to manage everything it holds for you — no password to remember.

Open the subscriber portal

From the portal you can unsubscribe, and if you are a paying subscriber you can also cancel, pause it for a while, change the card it charges, or read back through past invoices.

What happens next

  • The daily email stops. You may still receive one already on its way.
  • A paid subscription stops renewing, so you are not charged again.
  • You keep the analysis until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • Your address is removed from the list. Nothing is kept to mail you later.

Prefer to just ask?

Write to us from the address you subscribed with and we will do it by hand and reply when it is done. The link below prepares that email; it does not remove you on its own.

Ask us to remove you

If your email app does not open, write to info@sharperside.com. Please do not send card details.
