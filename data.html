The cheapest check that the parser is honest

Aggregates across everything ingested land where football says they should. If the reader were silently dropping matches, these would drift:

Silent drops are the failure mode. The score field arrives in three different shapes in the wild. Reading only the first loses whole seasons — and they arrive as unplayed fixtures rather than as an error, so a model trained on the survivors looks perfectly healthy. Nothing about it is visible except in aggregates like these.

Cached for as long as the answer holds, and no longer

A finished match's statistics are the same tomorrow as today. A fixture list is not. The rule is not cache less — it is cache for exactly as long as the answer cannot have changed:

EndpointTrusted forWhy
Statistics, lineups, leaguesfor eversettled once played
Fixtures15 mincarries live status and score
Injuries2 hoursteam news changes up to kick-off
Squads and player totals24 hourstransfers, accumulating totals
Quota statusnevermeaningless from a cache

This was learned expensively. A fixture list fetched at 08:56 said every match was unplayed with no goals — and went on saying it, so a match that had finished 0-2 was emailed as a forecast seven hours later.

The source that is blocked

This project began as something else: a harness to measure closing line value, which is the only honest early test of whether a forecasting model has any edge at all, because it scores a prediction against the final consensus price without any money moving. That brief was declined and the harness was never finished, for a reason worth recording rather than hiding.

The one free source carrying closing prices is blocked at the ISP. football-data.co.uk is intercepted by Etisalat and returns a redirect whose classification decodes to Gambling. TLS handshakes are reset; plain HTTP is redirected. The block is site-wide and is not rate-limiting.

Gambling is illegal for residents in the UAE, and the filter is that law appearing in the network. No attempt is made to route around it. Two modules survive from that phase because they are correct and independently useful, and neither is reachable from anything the daily run does.

This is why the product is what it is. It forecasts football matches and scores itself against baselines. It does not price anything, does not recommend anything, and could not do either even if it wanted to.
