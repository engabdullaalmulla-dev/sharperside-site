Subscription and billing

Sharper Side costs $15 a month. Payments are handled through Buttondown and renew monthly until cancelled. The Unsubscribe link at the foot of any Sharper Side email ends the emails and the renewal together; the subscriber portal does the same, and also lets a paying subscriber pause, change the card or read past invoices.

Privacy

No open or click tracking. The platform offers both. They are switched off, and the DNS record that click tracking needs was never added, so nobody here knows whether you opened this or which link you followed. The measurement that matters is whether the forecasts were right, and that is published.

Your address is used to send you this and nothing else.

Analysis, not advice.

Sharper Side is statistical analysis of football fixtures, published for information only. It is not advice of any kind and nothing in it is a recommendation to act, financially or otherwise. Football is uncertain, and no forecast here is a statement about what will happen.
